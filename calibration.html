The lesson: this was not a WebGL failure, a CORS problem, a CDN problem, or the phone's GPU refusing to cooperate. The code ran perfectly. It faithfully rendered a nearly-black material under dim light with the horizon fogged out. A correct render of a bad art direction is a black screen. Always look at the albedo and the light budget before blaming the device.
